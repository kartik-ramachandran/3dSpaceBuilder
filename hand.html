Hand mode

Explore the development as a hologram over your camera, using your hands. Pinch to select an apartment, make a fist to turn the model, and pull two fists apart to lift the floors away from each other.

  • The camera image is processed on this device and never uploaded.
  • Works best on a laptop in Chrome or Edge, with your hands well lit and about an arm's length away.
