Zoom in

One model, every level of detail

Most offerings are shown as a site plan, a price list and a brochure, each in a different place. Here they are the same model. Scroll, scale and select to move between levels without losing context.

01 · The whole site

Where the value sits, at a glance

Every apartment is coloured by its latest valuation rate, from light to dark blue. Apartments without a valuation are faded, so gaps in the evidence are as visible as the prices.

02 · One floor

Compare a level side by side

Switch to Select floor and the whole level lights up. The panel lists each apartment's size, rate and latest value, with the floor's average and range.

03 · One apartment

The evidence behind one number

Select an apartment for its latest valuation and rate per m², every valuation on record with quarter and purpose, and a placement confidence rating.

  • Hover any unit for its rate and quarter
  • Its valuations are highlighted on the trend chart
04 · The layout

The floor plan, with room sizes

Each apartment carries an indicative plan sized to its area: bedrooms, bath, kitchen, living and dining, and the balcony. Then Stand on the balcony to see its outlook.

05 · Step inside

A guided tour, room by room

Walk through a furnished home built from real materials, furniture and sky photographs. The tour stops in each room with its size and a highlight, and a live floor plan shows where you are.

06 · Pull back

The dollhouse view

Lift the roof to see both levels at once, or one floor at a time. Change the light from day to twilight and restyle the interior to test how the home presents.

Insights about the offering

From a price list to a read on the market

Seeing the offering in place shows patterns that a spreadsheet hides: which floors carry a premium, where the evidence is thin, and what the next tier up costs.

Trend

A trend surface, not an anecdote

Every valuation is plotted by quarter and floor. A degree-2 surface fitted by least squares shows the overall direction, and R² says how much of the variation it explains. Rotate it, fit the whole project or one building, and see where a selected apartment sits against the trend.

n = 115 valuations · R² = 0.78On the demo development: rates rise with floor and over time.

Coverage

See the gaps in the evidence

Unvalued apartments are faded, and the panel counts what is covered, so thin evidence is obvious before anyone relies on it.

73 of 96 apartments valuedDemo development, 2022 Q1 to 2025 Q4
